📖 How This Works
space-y-4 text-sm text-gray-700
font-semibold text-gray-900 mb-2
Why do markers appear in wrong positions?
The static map image shows a specific geographic area, but we need to tell the code exactly which latitude/longitude coordinates correspond to which pixels on the image. When these "bounds" are incorrect, markers appear offset from their actual locations.
How does calibration fix this?
By clicking on 2-4 known locations (places you can see labeled on the map) and entering their exact coordinates, the tool calculates the precise geographic bounds (min/max lat/lng) that match your image. This ensures all future markers align perfectly with the map.
